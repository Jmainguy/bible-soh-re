# Holy Bible

## Direction

A quiet reading room: warm, clear, considered. The identity should make Scripture feel approachable and give sustained reading the most space. Keep the public name **Holy Bible** and the address **bible.soh.re**. Header verse: **“Be diligent to present yourself approved to God as a workman who does not need to be ashamed, accurately handling the word of truth.”** with **2 Timothy 2:15 · NASB** beneath it. This is the full verse, using the wording from the site’s NASB translation.

This brand system is applied to the local site, not yet deployed to production. The user favors the pocket Bible silhouette and requested a more dimensional finish inspired by Flowboard and Babo Violent's logos.

## Dimensional logo treatment

Binding refinement: the user found the first dimensional concept's left strip looked like a flap. The [revised bound Bible](docs/brand/bible-dimensional-bound-v2.png) uses a three-quarter view, a continuous cover wrapping around the spine, and a page block visibly enclosed between covers. Preserve this credible construction in future versions; avoid a separate padded panel on the front. The user approved this revision; it is now the header and favicon asset in the local site.

The latest [dimensional Bible concept](docs/brand/bible-pocket-dimensional.png) preserves the pocket Bible shape, adding pine leather, a brushed brass cross, ivory page edges and a woven ribbon. Use restrained directional highlights and tactile materials to create depth. The user liked the underlying silhouette; this material treatment is a new proposal.

Use this detailed treatment for larger branding placements. Keep a simplified flat companion for very small icons, where leather grain and page detail disappear. Export web-sized assets from the original master rather than enlarging small exports. Inspect edges against both ivory and pine backgrounds before production use. The generated file's actual dimensions, rather than the requested prompt size, determine its available resolution.

| Ivory | `#F6F3EB` | Header and surrounding canvas |
| Paper | `#FFFEF9` | Reading surface |
| Ink | `#29332F` | Scripture and body text |
| Slate | `#5D6861` | Supporting labels |
| Brass | `#987746` | Small decorative details, never small text |
| Rule | `#D8DDD5` | Quiet dividers |

Use ivory as the main header background with pine text. Remove the heavy blue band and large header shadow. Reserve solid pine for primary actions and occasional supporting panels. Brass is a sparing accent, not a second dominant color. Preserve visible focus outlines and verify contrast for each implemented foreground/background pairing.

## Typography

- Masthead: Georgia, regular weight, with a restrained editorial feel. Desktop 30–34px; mobile 24–28px. Normal case: “Holy Bible.” Avoid bold, all caps, decorative scripts, and wide letter spacing.
- Scripture: retain EB Garamond with Georgia fallback, 20–22px and approximately 1.65 line height. Left align; avoid forced justification on narrow screens. Aim for 60–70 characters per line.
- Controls and metadata: system sans-serif, 14–16px, medium weight only where hierarchy requires it.
- Keep the masthead stable when changing translations. Show the translation name in a separate secondary label or selector, rather than appending a long name to the brand title.

## Logo explorations

The user prefers a recognizable Bible. The initial abstract open-book mark looked like two loose pieces of paper. Do not develop that disconnected silhouette further: show a real binding, connected cover, or unmistakable closed Bible.

These generated PNGs are concept artwork, not final vector masters. Preserve them as reference. After selecting a direction, simplify contours and verify the actual silhouette at 16, 24, 32 and 48px before making the favicon. The first option has more detail than a small icon needs. The third has the clearest compact structure.

Use one chosen mark consistently across the reader, sign-in pages, profile, groups and sharing artwork. Pair it with live “Holy Bible” text; do not bake the masthead into the image. Allow clear space of at least one quarter of the symbol height. Never stretch the logo or place pine artwork on a dark background; prepare a separate light version when needed.

## Header and reading layout

An ivory header, a thin bottom rule, and a left-aligned mark plus serif masthead. On desktop, place the translation selector and account controls to the right. On mobile, let controls wrap into a second row rather than shrinking the title. Keep the logo around 32–40px in visual height, allowing for the source artwork's margins.

The reading page should feel like good typesetting: generous margins, a restrained chapter heading, small verse numbers and no decorative paper textures. Highlight active navigation with a pale pine tint and a clear label. Keep notes and study controls visually secondary to the passage.

## Voice

Plain, welcoming and precise. Use “Continue reading,” “Choose a translation,” and “Your notes.” Avoid promotional claims, gamified spiritual progress, or implying the application speaks with religious authority. Clearly distinguish Bible text from reader comments and application guidance.
